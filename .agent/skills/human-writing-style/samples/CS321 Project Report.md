## **I. Introduction** 

This report reflects on my experience as a technical lead and primary contributor during this semester's software engineering project. Taking on both development and project management responsibilities provided unique insights into the challenges and opportunities of team-based software development. The following sections detail my contributions across different project phases and share key learnings about technical leadership, team dynamics, and project management.

## **II. Project Experience**

### **A. Project Contributions**

During Sprint 0, the initial learning phase, I led with the technical research and setting up the boilerplates for the project. The majority of time was spent on evaluating different speech-to-text APIs, AI models, database options, and other tools to figure out what would work best for our project. I also defined the functional requirements of our system's core components and documented detailed specs for the real-time processing system, WebSocket integration, and feedback mechanisms.

In our first implementation Sprint 1, I focused on defining the foundation architecture of the system. This directly contributed to the second project deliverable, where I documented the client-server architecture pattern which included WebSocket architecture for handling streaming data and defined integration patterns for external services. During this sprint, I also began implementing core features including the code editor integration and initial AI interview simulation components.

During the second sprint, I focused on creating the technical specifications for our backend systems such as database schema, developed the API endpoints structure, and created the WebSocket protocol for real-time communication. In addition with the speech-to-text functionality and AI feedback system while documenting these designs for our third project deliverable.

During the final sprint, I developed the majority of the core system components, along with documenting the implementation deliverable. My work included refining the AI interviewer behavior, backend API endpoints, implementing database integration, WebSocket infrastructure, and enhancing the real-time feedback system, as well as different parts of the UI components and user interaction.

Following Sprint 3, I focused on testing and documentation for our final project deliverable. This consisted of test plans and various testing strategies for both the backend and frontend components. This included writing unit tests for utility functions, integration tests for API endpoints, and end-to-end tests for WebSocket communications.

### **B. Team Project Learnings**

This project provided valuable but challenging lessons about team dynamics in software development. As the primary contributor handling both technical implementation and project management, I was able to take away the importance of early assessment of team capacity and commitment levels. This project taught me how important it is to have realistic expectations about team contributions and to adapt project scope accordingly. Although the lack of consistent team involvement created significant challenges, it pushed me to develop stronger technical independence and problem-solving skills. In addition to that, there was uneven technical expertise among the team members, this also taught me to adapt my communication style based on their knowledge and make sure to maintain detailed documentation in order for all team members to understand the system's architecture when they go through the source code.

### **C. Project Leadership Experience**

As the main contributor, as well as project manager, there were a lot of challenges in balancing technical implementation with team coordination. Despite attempts to establish clear communication channels and workload distribution, I found that managing a team with varying levels of commitment and availability required significant adaptation and flexibility. The experience taught me that effective leadership isn't about technical expertise, or being able to delegate tasks to team members, rather it’s more about understanding and adapting to others’ circumstances while maintaining project progress.

### **D. Team Meeting Dynamics**

## Our team struggled with consistent meeting attendance throughout most of the semester, despite attempts to establish regular weekly meetings. This inconsistency significantly impacted our ability to maintain effective team communication and collaborative problem-solving. However, during the final week before the demo, we experienced a brief but productive period of team collaboration (with the exception of one member). This intensive period proved valuable, as I learned to break down complex technical concepts quickly and delegate tasks effectively under time constraints. 

### **E. Project Successes**

The primary success of the project was the technical implementation and functionality of the final product, which met the intended requirements and performed as envisioned. Additionally, the team demonstrated strong presentation skills during the final demo, effectively communicating the project's value and features despite limited involvement in the development phases. 

### **F. Project Challenges**

The most significant challenge was the lack of consistent team involvement throughout the development process. I found myself responsible for both technical implementation and project documentation, often having to revise others' contributions to project deliverables. Taking on many responsibilities led to substantial time management challenges, particularly in maintaining comprehensive documentation and planning while implementing the technical features. The limited team participation until the final week meant that most development decisions and implementations fell solely on my shoulders.

### **G. Future Recommendations**

Looking back at this project, I would definitely implement more robust risk management strategies such as early potential risks, team availability constraints and technical expertise gaps, which would have allowed for much better planning.

Another key learning is realistic project scoping. In future projects, along with employing risk management strategies, I would begin with a thorough assessment of the team's collective capacity and technical capabilities before finalizing the project scope in order to ensure project ambitions are scaled based on actual team resources rather than ideal scenarios. 

I would also prioritize creating more detailed technical documentation earlier in the project lifecycle to address the challenges with uneven technical expertise. This documentation would include detailed setup guides for development environments, recorded technical walkthroughs of complex features and solutions to common issues and frequently asked questions. Serving as a living document for team members, reducing the learning curve for complex features. 

Furthermore, I would establish clearer accountability measures, including regular progress check-ins and specific deliverable requirements for each team member. While maintaining flexibility for personal circumstances, having clear expectations would help ensure more consistent participation throughout the project lifecycle.

### **H. Advice for Future Teams**

Based on my experience, I would advise future teams to have frank discussions about commitment levels and time availability for the project. Teams should establish clear accountability measures and realistic project scopes based on team capacity. It's crucial to understand that while technical skills are important, team commitment and consistent communication are equally, if not, more important for the project success.

## **III. Conclusion**

While this semester presented significant challenges in team coordination and workload distribution, it provided valuable lessons in technical leadership and project management. The experience of independently driving both development and documentation while attempting to foster team engagement has shaped my understanding of effective software engineering practices. These insights about risk management, project scoping, and team dynamics will greatly contribute to my approach to future software development projects
