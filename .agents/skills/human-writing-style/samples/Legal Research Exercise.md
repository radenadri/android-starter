In this case, a trade group called the Interactive Media Entertainment & Gaming Association sued the Attorney General of the United States. The group argued that the Unlawful Internet Gambling Enforcement Act was too vague, saying the law wasn’t clear enough for people to know what was actually illegal. The case went to the Third Circuit Court of Appeals, where the judges disagreed with the group as they stated that the law was clear enough to give people fair warning about what it banned. Effectively, the government won the case.
