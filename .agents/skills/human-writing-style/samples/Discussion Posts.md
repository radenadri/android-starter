## Ethics Discussion: Smart Home Devices

One topic I believe that should fall under the category of ethical issues would be smart home devices. This exposes problems with privacy as some of these devices can use the Wi-Fi signals themselves to see movement or even figure out the layout of your house. This raises concerns as to whether it is ethical for companies to give us technology that makes life more convenient, meanwhile creating risk that they could spy on us or misuse our personal information.

## Ethics Discussion: Rule Utilitarianism Application

Rule utilitarianism would approach this issue by asking what rule, if applied to all companies, would produce the greatest overall good. A possible rule is that companies should always get clear and informed consent before collecting or sharing smart home data. To figure out what's the right ethical outcome, a Rule Utilitarian would first brainstorm different competing rules that could apply to every company, and compare those to opposite rules. The next crucial step is to imagine separate worlds where each of these rules is the law for every, then weigh all consequences, calculate the total good and subtract from total bad. The rule that creates a world with the most overall good and least harm for the greatest number of people is the one that should be followed.
